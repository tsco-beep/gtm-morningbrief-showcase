The Morning Brief.

Every morning, before the rep logs in, a ranked brief of exactly what to do today — top priorities, deals at risk, follow-ups due, and one territory insight — delivered to Slack, every flag explained.

The problem

Sellers lose the first 30–45 minutes of every day deciding what to do — and often decide badly, chasing the loud deal instead of the slipping one.

Worse, deals die silently: nobody notices a champion went quiet until it's too late, and managers only see the risk at the QBR.

How it works

  1. Pull each rep's open deals, activity, and today's calendar.
  2. Score every deal for risk; flag the ones that are slipping.
  3. Surface follow-ups due and prep for today's meetings.
  4. Rank into a top 5 — capped, explained, actionable.
  5. Post to Slack at 7:00am, before the day starts.

The part most demos skip: how it decides what's "at risk".

A transparent, tunable risk score — not a black box. This is what an engineer builds from and an interviewer can't poke holes in.

SignalWhy it mattersWeight
Days since last activitySilence is the #1 slip predictor30%
Close date vs. stageNear close but early stage = danger25%
Days stalled in stageStuck deals quietly die20%
Champion engagement decayOpens / replies trending down15%
Single-threadedOne contact = one point of failure10%

Scores normalize 0–100 and are weighted by deal size, so a big quiet deal outranks a small one. Final daily priority ≈ impact × urgency, capped at five items.
